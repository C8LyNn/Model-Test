# Two-deck ship implementation plan

**Goal:** Remove deck 0 completely in a new scene while preserving two-deck play, essential services, evacuation and previous versions.

**Architecture:** Remove all deck-0 modules, zones, gates and lower wall segments. Move a compact selection of reactors, purification equipment, hydroponic plants, supplies and the shuttle into existing deck-1 sectors, preserving the six shared-height rooms and the deck-2 chase route. Reuse the modular shuttle gate in the remaining cargo/hangar sector. Seal the six lift shafts below deck 1, remove floor-0 buttons and landings, and make lift and overview controls reject unavailable deck 0 without changing older scenes.

**Checks:** Manifest and Unity tests must reject deck-0 modules and requests, exercise both remaining lift levels including cargo lifts, verify sector entrances and chase route, audit the relocated shuttle's fully open and closed sweep and moved equipment collisions, and inspect the scene visually. Old scenes stay available.
